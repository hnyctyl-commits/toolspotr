⭐ Favicon Generator

Generate favicon PNG and ICO files from an image or text/emoji. Download all standard sizes (16, 32, 48, 180, 512) in one click.

What is a favicon and which sizes do I need?

A favicon is the small icon shown in browser tabs, bookmarks and search results. Most sites need at least 16×16, 32×32 and 48×48 PNG files, plus a 180×180 apple-touch-icon for iOS home screens and a 512×512 version for Android/PWA manifests. This generator creates every standard size from either text/emoji or an uploaded logo image — all rendered locally on a canvas, so nothing is uploaded.
