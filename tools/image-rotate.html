🔄 Image Rotate & Flip

Rotate images 90°, 180° or 270° and flip them horizontally or vertically. Preview the result and download as PNG or JPG.

Rotating and flipping images correctly

Rotation turns the whole image clockwise, while flipping mirrors it along an axis — useful for correcting selfies taken in a mirror, scanned pages that came out sideways, or photos with the wrong EXIF orientation. Rotate by 90° steps for landscape/portrait fixes, and use the flip buttons for mirrored text or reversed logos. The result is re-encoded as a clean PNG with no metadata, so it works everywhere.
