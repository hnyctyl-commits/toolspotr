How aspect ratios work

An aspect ratio describes the proportional relationship between width and height. 16:9 (widescreen) is standard for YouTube and most monitors, 4:3 for older TV and presentation slides, 1:1 for Instagram and profile pictures, and 9:16 for vertical video such as Reels, Shorts and TikTok. Keeping the ratio while changing size prevents stretched or squashed images — enter one new dimension and the other is calculated automatically.
