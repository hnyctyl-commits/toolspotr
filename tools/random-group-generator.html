👥 Random Group Generator

Split a list of names into random teams or groups. Choose the number of groups and copy the result — perfect for classrooms and workshops.

Why random grouping matters

Randomly assigning people to teams removes bias and the awkwardness of picking partners, which is why teachers, trainers and event organisers rely on group generators. This tool uses cryptographic randomness so the split is genuinely unpredictable, distributes members as evenly as possible, and never sends your list anywhere — everything happens in the browser. Use it for classroom activities, team-building, study groups, seating plans or tournament brackets.
