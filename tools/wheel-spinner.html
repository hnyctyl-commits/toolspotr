🎡 Wheel Spinner

Spin a colorful wheel to pick a random winner from your list. Add names or options, spin, and see the result instantly. Free and fair.

A fair way to make random choices

A spinning wheel is a transparent, engaging way to pick a winner, choose a restaurant, assign tasks or run classroom activities. Results are generated with the browser’s cryptographic random number generator, so each entry has an equal chance. Everything stays on your device — the tool works offline once the page has loaded, making it ideal for live events, streams and classrooms.
