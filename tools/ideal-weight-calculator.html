⚖️ Ideal Weight Calculator

Calculate your ideal body weight using the Devine, Robinson, Miller and Hamwi formulas, plus your healthy BMI weight range.

What is a healthy weight?

“Ideal weight” formulas such as Devine, Robinson, Miller and Hamwi were designed for medical dosing and give slightly different answers — the spread above is normal and any value inside the healthy BMI range is generally appropriate for your height. BMI itself ignores muscle mass, so athletes often sit above the range while remaining healthy. Use this as a general reference, not medical advice, and talk to a healthcare professional about individual targets.
