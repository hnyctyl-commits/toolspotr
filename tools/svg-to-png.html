🖼️ SVG to PNG

Convert SVG vector graphics to PNG images at any resolution. Upload or paste SVG code, choose size and background, download instantly.

When should you convert SVG to PNG?

SVG is a vector format: it stays sharp at any size and is perfect for logos, icons and illustrations. PNG is a raster format required by many platforms that do not accept SVG — email signatures, social media uploads, Word/PowerPoint documents, and most app stores. Converting at a large resolution (1024px or more) keeps the result crisp. Use the transparent option for logos you plan to place on coloured backgrounds.
