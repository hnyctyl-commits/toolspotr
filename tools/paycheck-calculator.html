💵 Paycheck Calculator

Estimate your take-home pay. Enter your salary and tax rate to see net pay per period, month and year, plus total deductions.

How take-home pay is calculated

Your gross pay is what you earn before deductions; net pay is what actually reaches your bank account. Tax withheld depends on your jurisdiction and bracket, while additional deductions cover pension contributions, health insurance, union dues or garnishments. Because rates vary widely by country and state, this calculator uses a single effective rate you provide rather than hard-coding any tax system. Divide the per-period figures by your hours to see your real hourly rate.
